Game: finance101



A 2D game that allows player to make financial decisions based on different phases of life. Watching their net worth grow and fall with there own decisions. game is Build on 5 phases: 

* Student Part timer
* Freelance
* Job
* Job 2 / different role
* Business


Player has to reach a specific net worth or more to advance to the next phase

Each phase player starts with living conditions:

Like for Student Part timer - players conditions are you are a student earning about XX,XXX (for example only lets say 18,000) a month from part time income. You  have to put 10,000 K a month for college fees. 5K a month towards travelling to college. You are left with 3K. Since you are starting out YOU HAE NO INVESTMENT OPTIONS OPEN. Goal: manage your money, since you are a student you dont have to worry about neccesities like rent, emi, medical or food. They are not the game challenge YET. But Goal is to reach a networth of 36k within 10 months game period. while mantaining the expenses and fun index (Basically how many luxury and fun stuff you did) at a score of 2500. Basically for stuff needed trade in favours. Learn social capital. Barter with friends with stuff (Like exchange your comics for a shirt and perfume to go on dates). Learn about borowwing and make smart decisions based on what you can afford. YOU CAN NOT INVEST BUT YOU ARE ALLOWED LOANS (-ve networth) with interest of 9.5% every month. decide what opportunities are good enough to put money in. How much to save. How many opportunities of fun to take on while which ones to miss out on.
